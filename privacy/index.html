Tabby Speak Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Tabby Speak is a macOS app made by AGI Ready for dictation and meeting notes. This policy covers the data it reads from Google and Microsoft accounts you connect.

What Tabby Speak reads

When you connect a Google or Microsoft account, Tabby Speak reads your calendar events (title, time, attendees, location and meeting links) with read-only access. It uses them to name your recorded meetings and to suggest speaker names.

Where the data goes

Calendar data is stored only on your Mac, in Tabby Speak's application folder. It is not sent to AGI Ready or to any other server. Sign-in tokens are kept in the macOS Keychain.

Tabby Speak does not sell, share or transfer Google user data, and does not use it for advertising or to train AI models. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

In Tabby Speak, open Settings › Calendars and click Remove next to an account. This deletes its tokens and cached events from your Mac. You can also revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions or in your Microsoft account settings.
